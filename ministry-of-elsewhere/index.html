Every world begins with a door.

The daily ritual

Open the door. Write today.

Ministry of Elsewhere gives you one calm place to write a small daily draft, tend the lore behind it, and shape those fragments into a novel.

No cloud account. No proprietary manuscript vault. Your worlds remain ordinary files in folders you choose.

Inside the Ministry

From the first 200 words to the finished book.

01

Write today

Begin or resume today’s dated draft in one step. Gentle goals and writing rhythm make consistency visible without punishing a missed day.

02

Know your world

Highlight a passage and add it to a character’s information. Keep characters, places, maps, timelines, and continuity evidence connected to the prose that made them matter.

03

Shape the novel

Arrange scenes and chapters through an outline or corkboard, split and merge safely, and keep planning notes out of the reading copy.

04

Carry it elsewhere

Compile to Markdown, plain text, EPUB, or a 6 × 9 inch print interior PDF. The source stays yours before and after export.

Local by design

Your files are the product—not our database.

The app works only with folders you choose. It does not require an account, analytics, advertising, or an internet connection. Private recovery and progress records remain on your Mac.

From a passage to a person

Keep what your writing reveals.

Highlight a detail in your draft, choose Add to character…, and pick the person it belongs to. Review the words before saving them into the character’s information, with a reference to the original writing. Undo is available after saving.

Before you install

This preview is ad-hoc signed but not yet Apple-notarized. macOS will ask you to approve it manually.

  1. Open the downloaded DMG and drag the app to Applications.
  2. Try to open Ministry of Elsewhere once.
  3. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, choose Open Anyway, then confirm.

Only download this preview from 13squirrels.netlify.app. A normal signed and notarized installation is planned for the public release.

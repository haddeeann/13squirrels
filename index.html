Meet Vela

A calendar for how your brain actually works.

Most calendars assume you know when everything happens. Vela assumes you don't — and that's okay.

Capture first, schedule later. Brain dump your thoughts, then organize them when you're ready. No forced dates, no required fields, no friction.

Built for the way ADHD brains experience time — not the way productivity apps think they should.

The three building blocks

Events

Appointments and commitments with a time and a place. Vela makes sure you actually notice them.

Tasks

Things to do — with or without a deadline. Capture them now, schedule them when you're ready.

Anchors

The daily patterns that hold your life together — sleep, meals, workouts. Vela builds your day around them.

What makes Vela different

  • Brain Dump Capture

    Speak or type your thoughts. No dates required. Schedule them when you're ready.

  • Full-Screen Alarms

    Not a polite notification you'll swipe away. Vela demands your attention when it matters.

  • Sleep-Aware Timeline

    Set a sleep anchor and your daily view collapses those hours automatically. Your calendar reflects your actual life.

  • Quick Add with Natural Language

    Type "dentist Thursday at 2pm" and Vela understands. Title, date, time — parsed and pre-filled.

  • Designed for ADHD

    Calm colors, meaningful structure, zero unnecessary friction. Every design decision filtered through one question: does this help an ADHD brain?

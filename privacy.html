Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. The Short Version

Vela stores everything on your device. We don't have a server. We don't collect your data. We don't sell anything to anyone. That's genuinely it.

2. What Data Vela Stores

  • Calendar entries (events, tasks, anchors)
  • Alarm and timer settings
  • App preferences (appearance, notification settings)
  • Capture items (brain dump text and handwritten notes)

All of this lives in a SQLite database on your device. It never leaves your phone.

3. What Data We Collect

None. We don't have a backend server to collect data with. We don't use analytics. We don't track you. We don't have your email unless you gave it to us on our website voluntarily.

4. Permissions the App Requests

  • Notifications: to fire alarms, timers, and reminders
  • Microphone: for voice capture in Brain Dump (audio is processed on-device, never transmitted)
  • Vibration: for alarm haptic feedback

Vela does not access your contacts, camera, location, files, or any other device data.

5. Third Party Services

  • Expo Application Services (EAS): used for app updates. Expo may collect basic device information for update delivery. See Expo's privacy policy: https://expo.dev/privacy.
  • Audio CDN: alarm sounds may be loaded from an external URL. No user data is sent.

6. Children's Privacy

Vela is not designed for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children — because we don't collect data from anyone.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever start collecting data (for example, if we add cloud sync), we'll update this policy and let you know. But our philosophy is local-first, and we intend to keep it that way.

8. Contact

Questions? Reach us at hello@13squirrels.com.

Privacy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

The short version

Our apps are built to keep personal work on your device. We do not put advertising, behavioral analytics, or account tracking inside Vela or Ministry of Elsewhere. Each product has a few different storage and service boundaries, explained below.

•••

Ministry of Elsewhere

Ministry of Elsewhere works with ordinary project folders that you explicitly choose. Manuscripts, daily drafts, lore, timelines, maps, and portable project metadata remain in those folders.

The app also keeps limited private state on your Mac:

  • settings and recent-project shortcuts;
  • workspace and navigation state;
  • daily goals, word-credit history, and corrections; and
  • recovery drafts used to protect interrupted work.

The current app has no account, analytics, advertising, cloud sync, or automatic updater. It does not send project text or app-local writing records to 13 Squirrels Studio.

•••

Vela

Vela stores its working data on your device, including:

  • calendar entries, events, tasks, and anchors;
  • alarm and timer settings;
  • appearance and notification preferences; and
  • brain-dump text and handwritten notes.

Vela may request notifications for reminders, vibration for alarm feedback, and microphone access for voice capture. Voice capture is processed on-device. Vela does not request your contacts, camera, or location.

•••

Website and outside services

This website and its downloads are hosted by Netlify. Like most web hosts, Netlify may process standard request information such as an IP address, browser details, requested page, and time of access to operate and secure the service. We have not added advertising trackers or behavioral analytics to this site. See Netlify’s privacy statement for its practices.

Vela uses Expo Application Services to deliver app updates; Expo may process basic device information needed for that delivery. See Expo’s privacy policy. Vela may also load alarm sounds from a content-delivery network. Ministry of Elsewhere does not currently use either service.

•••

Information you choose to send

If you email us or voluntarily join a future beta list, we receive the contact information and message you provide. We use it to respond or provide the updates you requested, not for advertising profiles or sale to data brokers.

•••

Changes and contact

If a product later adds cloud sync, accounts, analytics, or another meaningful data flow, we will update this page before representing the old privacy boundary as current.

Questions can be sent to hello@13squirrels.com.
